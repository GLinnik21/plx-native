Artwork credits

The screenshots on this site show PlxNative browsing a demo library made entirely of openly licensed and public-domain works: open movies by Blender Studio and others.

Each work is listed with its author, licence, source and the changes made. Every picture was scaled and cropped to the app’s layout, and the screenshots themselves are cropped and resized for this site. Nothing was redrawn or otherwise altered except where noted.
