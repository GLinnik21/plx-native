# v0.9.0 — the Plex Watchlist, a Library that opens on the grid, and Collections you can find

This release adds the Plex Watchlist, makes the Library open on your library itself instead of on a page of recommendations, and puts Collections one press away from the Library header. Underneath that, subtitles are drawn by one renderer for every text format, more video and audio files direct-play, PlxNative remembers your audio and subtitle choices from episode to episode, and it speaks Italian, French, German, European Portuguese and Hungarian. It is the first release since 0.8.0, which was cut on 2026-10-07.

## What's new

- **The Plex Watchlist.** A bookmark tab sits before Search in the top bar and opens your plex.tv Watchlist as a grid, with one Sort & Filter pill for Type, Sort and Show. Movies and shows have a bookmark button on their page and on the Home hero, and the hold menu on a movie or show offers Add to Watchlist or Remove from Watchlist (on an episode or a season, for its show). A title you have not got on any of your servers opens a catalog page with its status and an Add to Watchlist or On Watchlist button and no Play; on the grid it wears a mark and the caption Not on your servers, and titles that are listed but not out yet read as coming soon. The list belongs to your plex.tv profile, not to a server, so adding or removing a title changes your real Watchlist, the same one the Plex apps on your phone show.
- **A Library that opens on the library.** The Library page is now always the full A to Z grid of posters: the sub-tabs and the All heading are gone, and the recommended shelves no longer sit above the grid. One header row carries the library names (with two or more libraries, the ones that do not fit fold into a **+N** menu, and a library switches on OK, never just by passing over it), then **Collections**, **Recommended** and **Sort & Filter**. When a filter is on, a read-out such as 15 of 16 films sits beside it. **Recommended** opens the server's shelves for that library on a page of their own, and BACK returns to the grid. Your sort, Unwatched switch, genre and listing type are still remembered for every library, per profile.
- **Under posters.** Settings → Libraries → Under posters chooses what is written under a poster: a Title switch and one line of Year and length, Genre, Video, Age rating and score, or Nothing. It applies to every poster in the app, Home, Library, Collections, Search, a person's films, the Watchlist and the shelves on a title's page, with a live preview in the setting.
- **Collections are easier to find.** Beside the Library header's Sort & Filter, **Collections** shows how many the library has and opens them as a grid; it appears once the server has said how many there are and is left out for a library without any. A single collection is still found in Search, from a collection shelf's heading on Home and on a library's Recommended page, and from the collection row on a movie's own page. A collection page names the page you came from above its title.
- **Open a Plex title from another app.** Another app on the television, such as a launcher's Continue Watching row, can ask PlxNative to open a title's page or to start playing it, by passing the server and the item in the launch parameters. This means any app on your television that can launch apps can do that, and a link that arrives during playback ends the film first; the details are in [Launch links](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/launch-links.md). Sign-in, the profile picker and onboarding are never interrupted: the link waits behind them, and once someone signs in, a link that names its server opens on that account while one that names no server is dropped. A running app picks the link up only where LG's own SDL library has the hook PlxNative uses, which per the firmware inventories is platform release 7.4 and later (measured on a 2022 LG C2 on platform release 9.2.2); everywhere else it works when the app is closed first.
- **One tile per show in Continue Watching.** If your own server and a friend's server both hold a show, Continue Watching now shows it once, as Plex Web does. A Continue Watching tile for an episode now reads like S3 · E4 · 18m left, and with Continue Watching set to Open details, OK on it opens the show (see Changed). Every show's page now opens on the season of the episode you are up to, with that episode in view, instead of on the first season.
- **PlxNative remembers your tracks per show.** An audio or subtitle choice you make in an episode is remembered for that show and applied to its next episodes, including a remembered external subtitle and a new **Forced only** row in the Subtitles menu. The choice is also written back to Plex, so your other Plex apps open the episode with the same tracks.
- **Text subtitles through one renderer.** SubRip, WebVTT and the other text formats are now drawn by the same renderer as ASS and SSA, with a bundled bold face for 22 more scripts, including Arabic, Hebrew, Thai and Devanagari. Subtitles in legacy encodings such as GBK, Big5, Shift_JIS and EUC-KR are detected per track instead of coming out as garbled characters. There is a Huge caption size above Extra large, and long captions wrap by width, capped at three quarters of the screen.
- **Scrubbing with a preview.** While you scrub, a still from the server's preview thumbnails floats above the knob, when the server has them. Left and Right, fast-forward and rewind only move the target; OK, Play or Play/Pause commits the jump and BACK cancels it. A held key starts at 60 times speed and doubles every second up to 480 times. Thanks to [dimitrije83](https://github.com/dimitrije83) for the preview thumbnails.
- **More files direct-play.** AV1 and VP9 direct-play on sets whose own decoder table lists them, and a VP9 file tagged HDR10 now switches the television to HDR. Dolby Vision Profile 7 files, the dual-layer kind on UHD Blu-ray remuxes, are converted to Profile 8.1 in the app and played as Dolby Vision; AV1 Dolby Vision declares itself; and a Dolby Vision file keeps its Dolby Vision when the server only copies the video, which is what Boost dialog and Normalize loudness do. Thanks to [RichiL10](https://github.com/RichiL10) for the Profile 7 conversion and to Tiago Pereira for the other two. A DTS track from a real Plex server direct-plays on a set whose table lists DTS, because the server's name for it, dca, is now understood. On webOS 5 and newer a seek no longer reloads the video.
- **Settings are per profile.** The playback settings, the subtitle size, position and look, Boost dialog and Normalize loudness, Under posters and the Home hero's trailer autoplay belong to each profile, and Settings names the profile it shows. A profile that has not chosen yet starts from the television-wide value. Automatic sign-in and Interface resolution stay television-wide.
- **Five new interface languages.** Italian, French, German, European Portuguese and Hungarian, alongside English, Spanish and Belarusian. Thanks to [Massisalo](https://github.com/Massisalo), [Yagz](https://github.com/Yagz), [U3knOwn](https://github.com/CE-Repo), [Tiago Pereira](https://github.com/tiper) and [Danzig](https://github.com/danzig666).
- **Emoji in titles and library names** are drawn instead of an empty box. Thanks to Tiago Pereira.
- **Sets on webOS 3.x, which are outside the supported range, got attention.** The prebuilt 0.8.0 closed on them at the first press of a remote's arrow key and again when a video began ([issue #862](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/862), [issue #249](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/249)): it called two functions their system does not have, and this release finds both at run time instead. A 2016 set on platform release 3.4.3 now signs in, browses and plays, a 2017 set on 3.9.0 reports the same on a nightly build, and Settings → System → Interface resolution (webOS 1 to 3 only) offers a lower resolution for smoother menus. Details are under Compatibility. Thanks to [selfhealer-png](https://github.com/selfhealer-png), [chriskoperdraad-tech](https://github.com/chriskoperdraad-tech), [Melvin Holt](https://github.com/MelvinHolt) and [Psychotonikum](https://github.com/Psychotonikum).
- **Also new.** The player's HUD truncates a long title and reveals it while paused ([infectiousstupidity](https://github.com/infectiousstupidity)), the no-server screen offers **Use another account**, the Quality row says what is actually playing, the video overlay and subtitles dim during HDR playback, the Magic Remote pointer hides after D-pad presses on webOS 3.x, and on a server you own, a page showing a title re-reads it when another Plex app plays or stops that title, and Continue Watching refreshes when it stops.

## Fixed

- **A film whose audio is stored ahead of its video never started.** An MKV with its audio about 48 seconds early ([issue #600](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/600)) and one whose audio starts about 11 seconds late now start and keep playing; thanks to [Danzig](https://github.com/danzig666) for the late-audio case.
- **A film no longer stays on its last frame after the credits** when its audio runs past the last picture. Thanks to Tiago Pereira.
- **With Boost dialog or Normalize loudness on, a 4K film could be shown at about 15 frames per second.** The server only copies the video then, and the film's own frame rate was not passed to the television.
- **Dolby Vision was lost with Boost dialog or Normalize loudness** ([issue #885](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/885)); it is kept now.
- **Boost dialog and Normalize loudness stayed unavailable for the rest of a film** after a subtitle the app cannot draw itself, such as a .sup file, was chosen; the server now burns that subtitle in while it converts the sound.
- **Changing the audio track while paused blanked the picture behind a Buffering spinner** until Play was pressed. The paused frame now shows, and preparing it no longer makes a short sound.
- **An automatically chosen forced subtitle switched subtitles off.** A track titled Forced or SDH was treated as a normal one when the account's language rule picked ([issue #828](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/828)).
- **Also available showed a wrong runtime** on show and season pages ([issue #814](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/814)); a show or season copy now shows none, and About's Runtime row is for movies and episodes only.
- **Play after coming back from a film started from the old position,** and an episode you had finished was not marked watched ([issue #575](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/575), [issue #680](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/680)). The page is re-read once your stop has reached the server.
- **Chinese, Japanese and Korean text could not be drawn on webOS 3.x and 4.x sets** ([issue #628](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/628)). Their system rejected the bundled font as it was; it is now cut down to a form they open, with no characters dropped.
- **Some embedded ASS and SSA subtitles never appeared**, because their headers end in a byte the renderer refused. Thanks to [Shakil Choudhury](https://github.com/sh-k-l).
- **Subtitle size and position were forgotten between restarts** ([issue #582](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/582)), **subtitles longer than three lines were cut off** ([issue #585](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/585)), and **a retry before the playback plan landed turned subtitles Off**.
- **A 4K H.264 file failed on some sets with a resource allocation error** ([issue #665](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/665)): the frame rate class of a file with a millisecond timebase was declared too high.
- **Direct play of a DTS-HD MA file hung** when the track list had not loaded ([issue #584](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/584)); the server now converts the audio.
- **Direct play over plain HTTP no longer stalls** when the server cuts the transfer or stops sending, and large MP4s with the index at the end start on webOS 3. Thanks to Tiago Pereira, [selfhealer-png](https://github.com/selfhealer-png) and [Melvin Holt](https://github.com/MelvinHolt).
- **HDR10+ files stalled the decoder** when the HDR10+ data shared a unit with other data. Thanks to Tiago Pereira.
- **Your answers to Connect without encryption?, subtitle timings and Library views survived signing out** ([issue #385](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/385)); sign-out now clears them, along with the remembered tracks and per-profile settings.
- **BACK over the playback controls left the film instead of hiding them,** a screensaver that took the decoder back left playback stuck, and a click with no control under the pointer did nothing after D-pad navigation. Thanks to dimitrije83 and [Frank Muller](https://github.com/frank-m).
- **Poster and focus glitches:** posters blanking when a menu closed over a Collection, a card snapping when a held key left it, Home briefly showing the shelf you had left when you moved down from the hero, the Home hero blinking black and changing title after you came back from a film, the show's logo drawn across the season pills while an episode had focus ([issue #679](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/679)), and a ghosted copy of an episode still when its menu opened.
- **Home and the Library lists reach their real end** (no fixed reach), Home's Recently Added loads in pages (thanks to Shakil Choudhury), and Library posters load ahead of a fast scroll. Key presses on Home and on Recommended, and a held key across a grid of posters, drop fewer frames.
- **Image cache on webOS 3.4** was off because the app could not find a writable directory; it now uses the one writable directory the television offers, sized to its free space.
- **Force Direct Play** names the audio codec it refuses instead of saying this audio format.

## Changed

- **Scrubbing asks for OK.** Releasing a held key no longer jumps; see What's new.
- **The Library's sub-tabs, All heading and TYPE menu are gone,** and the Library no longer scrolls the recommended shelves before the grid. Recommended and Collections are header buttons, and a TV library's Shows, Seasons and Episodes are under Sort & Filter → Show.
- **OK on a Continue Watching episode opens the show,** not the episode page, when Settings → Playback → Continue Watching is Open details (the default). Choose Play there to start the video as before.
- **Signing out clears more.** The remembered tracks, per-profile settings, subtitle timings, Library views and the last Library are removed with your sign-in, so one account's choices no longer carry to the next account on the same television.
- **The press-and-hold hint is shown once per television,** not once each time the app starts.
- **Crash and playback-error reports carry more fixed-value fields** (what failed, and the television's operating system and hardware, as codes and library names), for people who share reports. They are listed in the reporting question's preview and in [the privacy policy](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/PRIVACY.md); nobody is asked the reporting question again.
- **Two settings send a little more, and nothing is sent unless you turned them on.** With **Product analytics** on, leaving a screen now also reports broad classes of how smoothly the app ran on it: late frames, frozen frames, the app not responding, and a remote key pressed repeatedly with nothing happening; they are classes, never counts, durations, key names or anything you watched or searched for. With **Crash reports** on, a freeze of five seconds or more while the app is open on screen is reported like a crash (code addresses and a duration range), and one small record per stretch of use is sent even when nothing went wrong, so crashes can be counted per release: when it started, how long it lasted, how it ended, the Crash report ID, and the app version, with no content, account or server. Nobody is asked the questions again, because the choices are the same ones you already made; [the privacy policy](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) now describes all of it. Turning Crash reports off or signing out deletes the local session record and any waiting to be sent.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — 2019 LG sets.** One television: an LG 49SM9000PLA, which reports platform release 4.10.2 (LG markets this generation as webOS 4.5). Several changes in this version were checked on it while they were written; the audit says which, and that the release-level suites had not been run on it when this note was written.
- **Plays video, reported by someone else — 2025, 2021, 2019 and 2018 sets.** On 2026-10-21 an owner reported sign-in, browsing, search and movie playback on an LG OLED77B5PUA (2025, platform release 10.3.0) running the 0.8.0 release build: [discussion 906](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/906). On 2026-10-20 an owner reported sign-in, browsing, direct play of an MKV, resume and fast-forward on an LG 75UK6570AUA (2018, platform release 4.4.3) running 0.8.0: [discussion 882](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/882). Earlier reports stand for a 2019 set on LG's k5lp chip on v0.6.3 ([issue #74](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/74)) and an LG 65UP7560AUD (2021, platform release 6.5.2) on v0.2.1 ([issue #22](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/22)). None of these has been repeated on 0.9.0.
- **Sets on webOS 3.x are outside the supported range, but some now work on nightly builds.** The prebuilt 0.8.0 started on them and then died on the first D-pad press or the first video frame ([issue #862](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/862)); that is fixed in this release. On 2026-10-20, an owner reported sign-in by QR code, browsing, search and video playback on an LG 65UJ6200-UA (2017, platform release 3.9.0) on the 0.9.0 nightly of that day: [discussion 878](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/878). On 2026-10-21, an owner reported sign-in, browsing, direct play, a transcoded stream, subtitles and seeking on an LG OLED55C7V (2017, platform release 3.9.4) on a nightly, after crashes on 0.8.0: [discussion 900](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/900). A 2016 set on platform release 3.4.3 was tested by the author of the fix, selfhealer-png, on a build of the change itself: [pull request 578](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/pull/578). On a 2017 LG C7 on platform release 3.9.3, a build from source carrying the playback-start fix plays direct play: [pull request 700](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/pull/700). These are nightly, branch or source builds, not this release, and other 3.x sets are untested.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up is expected to start.** This binary's libraries and symbols are checked against the firmware images webosbrew has; the result for this build is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.9.0.md), including what it says for webOS 3.x. That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.

Separately, opt-in usage reports from 0.6.6 show video starting, directly and by transcode, on sets from platform release 4.4.2 up to 11.2.0. That is a count of starts, not a person watching a named set, so it moves none of the lines above.

If your set is not in the first group, [tell us what happened](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Known issues

- **Some 2019 sets on LG's k5lp or k3lp chip, installed through Developer Mode, cannot play video.** LG's sandbox can withhold a device the video path needs. The app says so instead of crashing, and on a rooted television the failure screen offers Repair, which has not been run on an affected set ([issue #74](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/74), closed with this still the case).
- **Sets on webOS 3.x are outside what is supported.** Seeking restarts the stream instead of jumping in place there, and menus can drop frames at 1920x1080. The crashes described under What's new were confirmed gone only on nightly and branch builds, not on this release's package.
- **A crash on televisions built on LG's k6hp chip**, seen only in opt-in crash reports and not reproduced, because no such set is available ([issue #174](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/174)).
- **Seeking without a reload on webOS 5 and newer has not been watched on one.** It goes through LG's public Seek call and was checked only by forcing that path on the 2019 set.
- **Dolby Vision Profile 7 conversion has not been watched on a television,** and AV1 and VP9 direct play depend on what the set's own decoder table says.
- **DTS audio has not been heard on a television.** The app sends DTS only when the set's codec table lists it, and DTS-HD MA 7.1 plays its core only.
- **Rare sequences can still leave a page empty,** and **Home can still miss a refresh while you flip the hero or move between shelves,** which reads as a small hitch. Neither was re-examined for this release.
- **Not every new feature has been seen on a television,** in particular server pages refreshing on their own, the Watchlist on a real account, and the per-profile settings across a restart. **The new Italian, French, German, European Portuguese and Hungarian wording has not been reviewed by us**, and some Belarusian and Spanish wording from 0.8.0 still wants a native speaker.
- **Auto quality has only been exercised in a laboratory**, and **servers behind Plex's relay, and servers that require an encrypted connection**, are reached but have not been watched end to end.

## Help test this release

**If you have a set on webOS 5 or newer,** seek in a direct-play film, in the middle and near the end, and tell us whether the picture carries on without a black flash. **If you have Dolby Vision Profile 7 files and a Dolby Vision set,** tell us whether the panel switches to Dolby Vision. **If you have a set on webOS 3.x,** tell us which platform release it reports in Settings → About This TV and whether the app starts, browses and plays. **If you use the Watchlist,** tell us whether a title you add on the television appears in the Plex app on your phone. [Open an issue](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Installing

Download **com.beb.plxnative_0.9.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.beb.plxnative_0.9.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. PlxNative is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later and bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and the libass subtitle renderer with its own libraries under their licences; the complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.9.0](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.9.0.md).
