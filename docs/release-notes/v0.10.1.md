# v0.10.1 — Home scrolling on LG's 2020 Realtek sets, seeks on a set with no displayed-frame counter, Continue Watching's first card, and Pause then Play

This patch release addresses six problems in 0.10.0, including a slow Home on LG's 2020 Realtek sets, choppy player menus, seeks that jumped back after four seconds on a set that reports no displayed frames, a Continue Watching row that opened past your newest cards, a Play that was lost while playback was still starting, and crash reports filed under the wrong version. There is nothing you need to do. It follows 0.10.0, which was cut on 2026-11-12.

## Fixed

- **Home scrolled slowly on LG's 2020 Realtek sets (the k6lp and k6hp chips, webOS 5.x).** The graphics driver on those sets checks whether one of LG's debug switch files exists at the start of every graphics call, and whether three others exist on draws, buffer swaps and shader changes, about 900 checks for each frame of Home in all. The app now answers those checks itself. In an earlier test on one such set, Home scrolled at about 38 frames a second. On one set with the k6lp chip, on platform release 5.6.2 and running a build of this change, the frames that took longer than 18 ms in a 55-second scroll of Home fell from about 1,600 to about 55, and the 95th-percentile frame time from 31 ms to 18 ms. No k6hp set was measured, though its driver is the same file in the firmware images that were checked, and sets on other chips make these checks far less often or not at all and were not measured. LG's debug switches no longer take effect inside this app, which nothing of ours used.
- **Audio and subtitle menus could stutter over playing video.** The player now limits its final colour correction to the parts of the overlay that need it. This reduces the graphics work when the screen dims behind the menu and preserves the brightness of subtitles and controls.
- **On a set that never reports displayed frames, every seek jumped back after about four seconds.** The picture resumed at the target, played for about four seconds, then showed Buffering and restarted at the target. 0.10.0 introduced it, and 0.9.0 seeked fine on the set that showed it, an LG OLED C8 on platform release 4.4.3 ([issue #1436](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/1436)). A seek now counts as landed on the playback clock alone when the set has never sent the count, and a set that does send it keeps 0.10.0's stricter check. Which other sets send no count is not known. Thanks to [robozdog](https://github.com/robozdog), who found the cause, wrote the patch and ran an equivalent two-line version on their own build of 0.10.0; this version of the fix has not been run on a television.
- **Continue Watching could open scrolled past your newest cards.** When your own server answered after a server shared with you, the row opened with the shared server's first card as its leftmost full card and focus on it, so the reporter needed five presses of Left to reach their most recent episode ([issue #1395](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/1395)). It now opens on the newest card, and a server with nothing to continue no longer leaves a placeholder before the first card. This was reproduced and fixed in the app's own tests and has not been seen on a television; which server answers first in the reporter's household was inferred from the code, not observed.
- **Pause then Play, pressed while playback was still starting or refilling after a seek, left it paused.** The Play was dropped, and later presses did nothing until you sought or stopped. It now resumes. On a television against a test server, three cases (Pause then Play during the first start, during a long wait after a seek, and a Pause before the seek) each resumed in one run, where without the fix, Pause then Play after a seek never resumed in two of two tries; a re-buffer in the middle of a streamed play and a quality change from the player menu were not tried.
- **A crash report left by an older build was sent as a crash of the newer one.** This concerns only the crash reports you opted in to. They are sent the next time the app starts, so after an update a crash recorded by 0.9.0 was filed under 0.10.0. A report whose record names a different build than the one sending it now carries no app version of its own, and says instead which version sent it and which build crashed, so it no longer counts against the release that uploads it; the crash log does not record the crashed build's version, so such a report belongs to no release rather than to the right one. Nothing new is collected, and [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) says what each report carries.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — 2019 LG sets.** One television: an LG 49SM9000PLA, which reports platform release 4.10.2 (LG markets this generation as webOS 4.5). Several changes in 0.10.0 were checked on it while they were written; no package of this release had been run on it when this note was written, and the audit says what was checked.
- **Plays video, reported by someone else — 2025, 2022, 2019 and 2018 sets.** On 2026-10-30 an owner reported sign-in, browsing, direct play, resume, seeking, HEVC or 4K and subtitles on an LG OLED55C24LA (2022, platform release 9.x) running 0.9.0, noting that Back can leave the app stuck on some pages: [discussion 1167](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/1167). On 2026-10-26 [cremor](https://github.com/cremor) reported sign-in, browsing, direct play, HDR or Dolby Vision, resume and subtitles on an LG OLED65C9PLA (2019, platform release 4.10.2) running 0.9.0: [discussion 1071](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/1071). On 2026-10-21 an owner reported playback on an LG OLED77B5PUA (2025, platform release 10.3.0) running 0.8.0: [discussion 906](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/906), and on 2026-10-20 another on an LG 75UK6570AUA (2018, platform release 4.4.3) running 0.8.0: [discussion 882](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/882). None of these ran 0.10.0 or 0.10.1.
- **Sets on webOS 3.x are outside the supported range, but some work on nightly builds.** On 2026-10-20 an owner reported playback on an LG 65UJ6200-UA (2017, platform release 3.9.0) on a 0.9.0 nightly: [discussion 878](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/878), and a contributor ([dimitrije83](https://github.com/dimitrije83)) made webOS 3 seek in place through LG's own Seek call, tested only on a set reporting platform release 3.9.3. Whether this release's package starts on them has not been confirmed on one.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up is expected to start.** This binary's libraries and symbols are checked against the firmware images webosbrew has; the result for this build is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.10.1.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.

If your set is not in the first group, [tell us what happened](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Known issues

These are 0.10.0's, which this release does not change.

- **Direct play can hang on Buffering on some webOS 5 and newer sets.** [Issue #1259](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/1259) tracks the open reports, among them a 60UN7310 (platform release 5.x, [issue #954](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/954)), and says the causes are not confirmed to be the same. The earlier report from an LG OLED55B2 (platform release 9.x, [issue #1096](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/1096)) was closed as fixed by a change already in 0.10.0, after a second set with the same failure direct-played with it; nobody has confirmed it on the B2 itself.
- **Some 2019 sets on LG's k5lp or k3lp chip, installed through Developer Mode, cannot play video.** LG's sandbox can withhold a device the video path needs. The app says so instead of crashing ([issue #74](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/74)).
- **Sets on webOS 3.x are outside what is supported.** Menus can drop frames at 1920x1080 there. [Issue #862](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/862), the report from a webOS 3.x set, was closed on 2026-11-12.
- **A crash on televisions built on LG's k6hp chip,** seen only in opt-in crash reports from 0.6.6, none from 0.7.0 or later, and not reproduced ([issue #174](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/174)).
- **The subtitle timing offset is missing a minus direction** ([issue #1284](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/1284)).
- **Back can leave some pages stuck,** reported on one 2022 set ([discussion 1167](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/discussions/1167)) and not reproduced here.
- **Most of 0.10.0's new features had not been seen on a television running that release's package,** in particular the HUD gallery, installing a package from an address, automatic intro skipping on a real Plex Pass account, the Playback engine setting's Experimental choice, and app-decoded audio on sets other than the development one. **The new Russian, Polish, Dutch and Catalan wording has not been reviewed by us.**
- **The Home administrator's Plex Pass for a managed profile has never been seen on a real managed account.** The rule is the maintainer's choice and was built against the documented answers; a full (not managed) Home member also takes the administrator's Pass because the household list cannot tell them apart.
- **Yellow subtitles are unseen on a television, and have one known cost:** white signs and typesetting in a styled subtitle turn yellow too, and how the yellow and its outline look on the panel and in HDR is unchecked.
- **Two Library symptoms were not seen to happen here:** the old layout flashing when you go Back to the grid, and focus after opening straight onto Collections; the changes for them are unconfirmed on a television.
- **Servers behind Plex's relay, and servers that require an encrypted connection,** are reached but have not been watched end to end.

## Installing

Download **com.beb.plxnative_0.10.1_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.beb.plxnative_0.10.1_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. PlxNative is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later and bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and the libass subtitle renderer with its own libraries under their licences; the complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.10.1](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.10.1.md).
