# v0.8.0 — subtitle search and styled subtitles, collections, and settings for how you watch

This is the first release from the main line since 0.7.0. You can now search for a subtitle and add it from the player, styled ASS and SSA subtitles keep their styling, Plex collections can be browsed, servers with Plex Pass get Boost dialog and Normalize loudness, and there are Settings for how you watch. Home and the Library no longer cut off your shelves, and the interface is available in Spanish and Belarusian. Everything fixed in 0.7.1 is included.

## If your server answers only without encryption: you will now be asked before PlxNative connects that way

Some servers answer on the home network only over plain HTTP. Earlier versions refused to send your Plex sign-in that way and stopped at "Couldn't sign in". 0.8.0 can connect, but only after you answer **Connect without encryption?** with yes, and only when every secure route to that server, Plex's relay included, has failed. The question appears on the sign-in screen, on the Home or Library screen that reports the failure, and in Settings → Unencrypted connections.

It is offered only for a server that plex.tv lists as being on the same network as the television, at a private numeric address. It is never offered for a remote address, for the relay, or for a server set to require secure connections.

**What to do:** nothing, unless you are asked. If you say yes, your Plex sign-in for that server travels without encryption across your home network, to that server only, so anyone who can observe that network could read it. Settings → Unencrypted connections turns it off again at once.

**What is fixed, and what is not:** the permission itself is never stored. It ends when you sign in or out, when you switch to a profile that does not use that server at that address, and when the app comes back from the background, and the app keeps retrying a secure connection and switches back as soon as one verifies. Not fixed: your answer, allowed or declined, is kept per server for your Plex account and is still on the television after you sign out or choose Delete all local data, although the privacy text says signing out removes it ([issue #385](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/385)). The same account signing in again gets its earlier answer back without being asked; another account is asked afresh.

## If OK on a Continue Watching card used to start the video: it now opens the page

OK on a card in a Continue Watching row, on Home or in a Library, now opens that movie or episode page by default. **What to do:** to get the old behaviour back, choose **Play** under Continue Watching in Settings → Playback → Video & playback. With the default, holding OK on the card opens a menu that leads with Continue or Play. Cards outside Continue Watching, the Home hero's Play button and the episode strip do not follow this setting.

## What's new

- **Search for subtitles.** While a video plays, the Subtitles menu ends with **Search for subtitles**. Pick a language, and OK on a result asks your server to download it; PlxNative turns it on when the server lists it. A result the item already has reads Added. Downloading adds the subtitle to that item in your server's library, so everyone who uses that library sees it, and your server may refuse it for an account that is not allowed to add subtitles. Long subtitle names scroll while focused. Thanks to [Frank Muller](https://github.com/frank-m).
- **Styled subtitles.** ASS and SSA subtitles keep their styles, positions, fonts and effects instead of showing as plain text, when a video plays directly or the server only repackages it on your local network. A server re-encode still draws subtitles into the picture itself.
- **A subtitle menu that scales.** Tracks are grouped by language, your own languages come first and the rest sit under **Other languages**, and picture-based subtitles carry a badge. **Style** sets size, position and colour, and **Timing** shifts captions earlier or later; the timing you chose is remembered for that item. Settings → Playback → Video & playback has default subtitle size and position. Subtitle timing began as a contribution from [Jacopo](https://github.com/jacksilv).
- **Collections.** A Plex collection opens its own page with its artwork, its item count, its summary and its members in your server's order. Find collections under **TYPE** in a library, in Search, from a collection's shelf heading on Home and in a Library, and from the collection row on a movie's own page. A collection without its own poster is drawn as a fan of its first three posters.
- **Boost dialog and Normalize loudness.** In the player's Audio menu, on a server that has Plex Pass, when the video plays at Original quality. The server does the work, which can mean converting the audio, and with a subtitle showing it draws that subtitle into the picture, so Style and Timing wait until you turn the option off. Nothing is shown on a server without Plex Pass.
- **Settings for how you watch.** Settings → Playback → Video & playback sets your default quality and Direct Play, which is Auto unless you choose Force or Disabled; Force asks you to confirm every time, because it can mean no sound, a wrong picture or a freeze. It also sets **Next episode** (the Up Next countdown, After credits, or Off) and **Skip interval**. Up Next now appears only for credits that run to the end of the video, so an after-credits scene is no longer skipped. **Audio & subtitles** shows and changes the audio and subtitle language preferences that plex.tv holds for the Plex profile you are using. DTS audio is now played by the television when it says it can decode it, instead of always being converted by the server.
- **Spanish and Belarusian.** PlxNative follows the television's language and offers a language choice in Settings → System → Language, which applies the next time you start the app; the page says how to restart it. Thanks to [Nacho](https://github.com/gnacho) for the Spanish translation and its review and to [Zmicier Kachkou](https://github.com/ZmicierKachkou) for reviewing the Belarusian.
- **The Library remembers your view, and nothing is cut off.** Each profile's sort, Unwatched switch, genre and listing type are remembered for every library. The type menu offers Seasons and Episodes in TV libraries, and Collections in both kinds. Home and the Library no longer stop at a small fixed number of shelves, and a shelf is shown in full or not at all.
- **Hold OK for options.** A resting Home card shows a short hint that holding OK opens its menu. It stops once you have opened an item menu, and comes back the next time you start the app.
- **Artwork is kept between runs.** Posters, backdrops, cast photos and profile pictures are stored in a bounded cache on the television so they do not download again after a restart. Signing out removes the cache.
- **Clearer sign-in problems.** The sign-in screen now says whether plex.tv or your server could not be reached, and account calls retry a few times before giving up. If your account has no server, the screen names the account you signed in as, so a wrong account is easy to spot; the name is shown on screen only and is never logged or sent. Failure screens carry a symbol and are centred.
- **Look and feel.** A focused tile looks like a raised pane of glass, menus are as wide as their content, the player's menus slide between pages, and Settings is reordered with About last.

## Fixed

- **A film whose default audio the television cannot decode played a different track instead, such as a commentary.** The server now converts the audio of the track you were meant to hear and copies the picture. The film page reads "Original · audio converted", and the Audio menu marks every track the television cannot decode as converted by your server.
- **A downloaded subtitle was lost the next time you played the film, and a subtitle flashed at the start.** The subtitle the server selected stays selected, and the app draws an embedded subtitle itself on a repackaged video on a local connection, so Style and Timing work there.
- **The screen froze after picking another audio track, subtitle or quality while a video played, until a slow server answered.** The server calls now happen off the screen's thread, and a seek during a transcode pauses the old picture under the Seeking spinner while the server prepares the new stream.
- **The who's-watching picker opened on the last profile instead of the first** when you have more than one, and **a correct PIN showed the profile list for a moment** before Home.
- **Posters that blinked back to grey** after startup, sign-in or a profile pick, **a row under the tab bar that lost its artwork one tile at a time**, and **a Home hero that blinked** when you flipped it left or right.
- **Focus that landed in the wrong place** in the Library (Down, Right, Up did not return to the tile above, and focus sometimes jumped to the All heading), plus a batch of smaller focus and layout defects across Settings, Library, the item pages and failure screens.
- **Home tiles drawn at the top of the screen over the hero** for a frame when the shelves arrived while a menu was open, and **a Home shelf heading that popped in** instead of sliding with the page.
- **A TV show page with many episodes ran slowly**, and moving between pages hitches less.
- **Everything fixed in 0.7.1:** servers whose certificate the television could not verify, which 0.7.0 reported as every profile having no access, and a television with a wrong clock reaching a server it had verified before. See the [0.7.1 notes](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/releases/tag/v0.7.1). New since 0.7.1: when a wrong clock stops the app reaching a server and it cannot help, the failed Home or Library screen now says the clock looks wrong and to connect the television to the internet once.

## Changed

- **The app's logs can now be read by the app's group.** The event, crash and error logs are mode 0640, so webOS Dev Manager can fetch them from a Developer Mode television that is not rooted and a bug report no longer needs root. The event log is scrubbed of tokens and local addresses before it is written, but a few lines written by the C part of the app and what the television's own libraries print skip that scrubbing, as [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/SECURITY.md) states, and another app on the same television that runs in PlxNative's group can read the files too. Read a log before you share it. This came in 0.7.1, so it is new only if you are updating from 0.7.0.
- **If you share error reports or analytics, a few more fixed-value fields can be sent** when your server refuses a playback: the server's refusal codes, the route the app tried, and the kind of video and audio, as short fixed codes, never the server's own wording, file names or addresses. Nobody is asked the reporting question again by this update. Sign-in steps are now held in memory while the question is unanswered, sent only if you answer yes, and dropped if you sign out. Every field is listed in the reporting question's preview and in [the privacy policy](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — 2019 LG sets.** One television: an LG 49SM9000PLA, which reports platform release 4.10.2 (LG markets this generation as webOS 4.5). Several changes in this version were checked on it while they were written; the audit says which, and which were not.
- **Plays video, reported by someone else — one 2019 set and one 2021 set.** [Robert](https://github.com/RobertSmits) got playback back on a 2019 set built on LG's k5lp chip after repairing its video sandbox, running v0.6.3, on 2026-09-11: [issue #74](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/74). [mariotaku](https://github.com/mariotaku) of webosbrew ran v0.2.1 on an LG 65UP7560AUD (platform release 6.5.2) on 2026-08-11 and got a picture: [issue #22](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/22). Neither report has been repeated on this version.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up is expected to start.** This binary's libraries and symbols are checked against the firmware images webosbrew has; the result for this build is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.8.0.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.
- **Does not start — webOS 3.9.2 and older.** Symbols the app needs are missing there, so the process is killed before anything appears.

Separately, opt-in usage reports from 0.6.6 show video starting, directly and by transcode, on sets from platform release 4.4.2 up to 11.2.0. That is a count of starts, not a person watching a named set, so it moves none of the lines above and has not been repeated on this version.

If your set is in the middle two groups, [tell us what happened](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Known issues

- **Some choices outlive signing out.** Your answers to Connect without encryption? ([issue #385](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/385)), and the Library views and subtitle timings added in this version, are kept in preferences that signing out does not clear. They hold server, library and item identifiers, not your sign-in.
- **Some 2019 sets on LG's k5lp or k3lp chip, installed through Developer Mode, cannot play video.** LG's sandbox can withhold a device the video path needs. The app says so instead of crashing, and on a rooted television the failure screen offers Repair, which has not been run on an affected set ([issue #74](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/74)).
- **2018 sets on firmware that reports platform release 3.9.3** sign in and browse, but video has never been seen to start on one, and no error is reported either ([issue #249](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/249)).
- **A crash on televisions built on LG's k6hp chip**, seen only in opt-in crash reports and not reproduced, because no such set is available ([issue #174](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/174)). No report from 0.7.0 or later had arrived when it was closed.
- **A library with many recommended shelves has no shortcut to its grid** ([issue #425](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/425)), now that no shelf is cut off.
- **DTS audio has not been heard on a television.** The app sends DTS to the television only when its own codec table lists it, and the pipeline accepts it, but nobody has confirmed sound or sync.
- **Several features were checked in the simulator or in tests only, not on a television:** collections, Continue Watching's new default, the Library's remembered view and the new sign-in messages. The wrong-clock toast has not been seen on a television either.
- **Some Spanish and Belarusian wording added late has not been reviewed by a native speaker**, including the new reporting-preview labels and the Home hold hint.
- **Auto quality has only been exercised in a laboratory**, and **servers behind Plex's relay, and servers that require an encrypted connection**, are reached but have not been watched end to end.

## Help test this release

**If you are updating from 0.7.0 or 0.7.1**, install over it and tell us whether you were still signed in, on the same profile. **If your server has Plex Pass**, turn Boost dialog on and then off in the middle of a film and tell us whether the picture and sound carry on and whether playback returns to direct play. **If you have collections**, open one from the Library's TYPE menu, from Search and from a movie page. **If you use Spanish or Belarusian**, tell us any wording that reads wrongly. **If you have DTS files and a television that decodes DTS**, tell us whether you hear them. [Open an issue](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Installing

Download **com.beb.plxnative_0.8.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.beb.plxnative_0.8.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. PlxNative is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later and bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and the libass subtitle renderer with its own libraries under their licences; the complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.8.0](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.8.0.md).
