## 3. Scrim in the page pass (§16.3, risk 3) — the one unproven claim

### What the before/after can and cannot be

The phase-9 tree did **not** have this bug live. Four call sites already put their dim in the page
pass by hand — `account_menu::draw_scrim()` and `item_menu::draw_scrim()` from `app/run.rs`'s page
closure, `about_panel::draw_scrim()` from `screens/detail/mod.rs` — and `tracks_panel` /
`alt_sources` rode `Popover::painter`, which draws the scrim as its first act so a CACHED host's
snapshot carries it. Phase 10 replaced all of that with one container-owned pass
(`ModalStack::draw_scrims`, `Screen::scrim`).

So this is a **refactor**, and the honest test is not "was it broken before" but **"does the
generalised mechanism reproduce what four hand-placed call sites did"**. A visible difference would
be the regression. That is what was measured.

Two numbers per pair, from `PIL`/`numpy` over the captured PNGs rather than by eye:
**(a)** the page OUTSIDE the panel must darken — the scrim is drawn at all; **(b)** the page UNDER
the panel, seen through the frosted glass, must be consistent with (a) — the scrim was in the
snapshot the glass sampled. The failure being guarded against shows up as a panel whose ground is
at FULL page brightness inside a dimmed screen.

### Method note — one control that had to be thrown away

The first attempt captured Home twice, from two boots, with `plxnative-heroidx=0` pinning the hero.
It reported a dim of 0.242 and **that number is wrong and is not used below**: the two frames were
different screens. The reference had a full-bleed hero backdrop and the shelf at the bottom; the
sheet frame had no backdrop and the shelf at the top. Almost all of that "dim" was a missing
backdrop. Home's hero rotates and its backdrop lands asynchronously, so **two boots of Home are not
a control**. The pairs below are taken either in ONE app session (capture, then open the surface
with a pointer click on the profile chip — the sheet freezes its host, so the second frame is the
first plus exactly the dim and the panel) or on the DETAIL page, whose content is static and where
two boots really are comparable.

### A real finding: the `plxnative-itemmenu` boot trigger is timing-fragile

The first card-menu attempt produced **17 heartbeats of bare `route=home`** — the surface never
opened — while `fps:item-menu` had passed with n=10 samples of `overlay=itemmenu` an hour earlier
on the same build. The cause is in `dev/scenarios.rs`, not in the surface:

* `grid_library_search_heroidx_arm` queues `HomeCmd::FocusGrid` **once**, at t0+400 ms, and sets
  `grid_tried` unconditionally.
* `itemmenu_arm` then needs focus to be ON THE GRID: `request_home_menu` "refuses while focus is on
  the strip rather than the grid". It retries until a **12 s ceiling**, then latches
  `itemmenu_tried = true` whether or not the panel appeared.

So on a boot where the Home catalog lands later than 400 ms — a COLD boot, which is what a capture
run does and what an fps suite running scenes back to back does not — focus never enters the grid,
every retry is refused, and the arm gives up silently. No log line is written.

This is **not** a phase-10 regression: phase 10 made this arm strictly more honest (its doc records
that "done means the SURFACE is up" is new in phase 10, replacing a latch on the enqueue). The
fragility is the single-shot `FocusGrid` underneath it. The card menu itself is fine — driven by
the real gesture (`plxnative-grid`, then the FIFO's `okdown` / >500 ms / `okup`) it opened first
time, for **12 heartbeats of `route=home overlay=itemmenu`**, and that is the capture recorded
above.

## 5. Host-side fix landed this session

**`tools/tv-session.sh` still named the routes phase 10 retired.** `tests/manifest.json` re-keyed
`home-acct-glass` and `item-menu`; this script did not move with them, so `--screen account` and
`--screen itemmenu` asked for `route=account` / `route=itemmenu`, which nothing prints any more.

The visible symptom was cosmetic — the call site is `|| true`, so a correct boot printed a red
`wanted route=account, got route=home`. **Correcting only the word would have been worse than
cosmetic**: `assert_route` greps `route=[a-z]+` alone, and every one of these surfaces sits over
Home, so `route=home` is true from the first heartbeat of any boot and the assertion would have
reported success for a boot whose trigger the app refused. An assertion that cannot fail is worse
than one that cries wolf.

Both halves landed together, watched red first (`ci/test_tv_session.py::HeartbeatWords`, 7 new
cases; the harness stubs `tvq` by running its command string against a real local event log, so
the grep under test is the script's own — the first draft echoed a heartbeat back instead and
bypassed the expression it was meant to grade). A second commit narrowed it: the overlay is
compared **only when the caller named one**, because `--screen detail=<rk>` with a panel trigger
armed beside it is the ordinary way to reach a page-owned panel and must not warn.

It earned its keep the same session: the failed card-menu boot above was reported as
`✗ wanted route=home overlay=itemmenu, got route=home` — exactly the case it was written for, and
exactly the case the old code would have called a success.
