## Summary

Plan to finish #42 Kodi-parity buffer-feed hardening. The remaining gaps collapse Kodi's four-way per-ES decoupling into one interleaved AuQueue drained by one main-thread feeder with one break, plus several missing Load/lifecycle seams. The keystone is the two-lane feed split (a): today the per-lane budget/slack constants in engine.rs::feed_stream (MAX_FEED_AHEAD_NS / AUDIO_SLACK_NS) are structurally dead because a single pts-ordered FIFO + single break means a video BufferFull or over-budget head stalls audio too, wedging the audioSync master clock. The specs' feeder_thread depends_on two_lane and two_lane depends_on feeder_thread; I break that cycle by landing the two-lane split FIRST on the existing main-thread pump (Engine fields stay main-thread-confined, per the two_lane spec), THEN moving feeding onto dedicated per-lane threads which migrates the per-lane fed-pts into SHARED atomics. Seek-reanchor cleanup and eof/unload build on the two-lane + feeder base; the esInfo dims/fps and per-codec audio blocks share a serde_json payload-builder refactor (esInfo first, audio second, so they don't rewrite the same JSON twice); setHdrInfo and the ACB pause/playing mirror are independent. Everything reuses existing mechanisms (aq byte-cap backpressure, the SendPtr worker-handoff, the abort+join teardown discipline, the sf_/acb_ seam, the log()/tests/run.py harness) rather than bolting on special cases.

## Dependency notes

The two specs form a cycle (two_lane_feed depends_on feeder_thread AND feeder_thread depends_on two_lane_queue). I break it as the task directs: land the two-lane split (step 1) FIRST on the existing main-thread pump, keeping the new per-lane fed-pts as main-thread-confined Engine fields (exactly what the two_lane spec says to do for this stage). Step 2 (feeder_thread) then moves feeding to dedicated per-lane threads and migrates those per-lane fed-pts into SHARED atomics + adds the seam mutex; it reuses step 1's per-lane state rather than reworking it. Step 3 (seek_reanchor) depends on BOTH 1 (per-lane fed-pts reset) and 2 (bounded reopen gap to make prime a gated fallback). Step 4 (load_timeout_abort) is independent but is sequenced before step 5 because eof_and_unload's synchronous Unload is only safe if Load can't wedge first, and its spec lists load_timeout_abort as a dependency. Step 5 (eof_and_unload) depends on 1 (EOS keyed to the video lane drain) and 4. Steps 6->7 must stay adjacent and ordered: esInfo introduces the serde_json payload builder that replaces the fragile .replace() chain, and audio_codec_blocks (7) reuses that builder: doing them out of order or in parallel would rewrite the same JSON assembly twice (bufferingCtrInfo tuning would touch it too; coordinate). Steps 8 (hdr) and 9 (acb pause/playing) are fully independent of the feed chain and of each other and can be scheduled whenever; they are last only because they are lower-risk/optional (HDR is belt-and-suspenders on a panel where in-band SEI already works). ALREADY MATCHED and NOT re-planned: in-process BUFFERSTREAM + ACB bind order; EAC3->'AC3 PLUS' string (engine.rs:219-223); the in-place seek reanchor primitive itself (setTimeToDecode/setContentInfo(ES)/sendSegmentEvent on webOS 4.5): step 3 only touches its residual behavioral divergences.

- webOS 4.5 BufferFull granularity: is the Feed 'BufferFull' reply per-esData (per-lane, which the whole two-lane retry model assumes) or a global 'some buffer full'? If global, per-lane retry busy-spins the full lane; confirm from the feed reply log on-device before relying on independent retry.
- Does webOS 4.5's closed Starfish actually consume esInfo.videoFpsValue/videoFpsScale/videoWidth/videoHeight and adaptiveStreaming.adaptiveResolution, or does it always read coded dims from the SPS and ignore these? (Determines whether step 6 has behavioral upside or is cosmetic.)

- Does webOS 4.5 emit PF_EVENT_TYPE_STR_STATE_UPDATE__UNLOADCOMPLETED, and is StarfishMediaAPIs::pushEOS present (non-weak) on this firmware? Step 5's 1s Unload cap degrades gracefully if UNLOADCOMPLETED is absent, but a missing pushEOS symbol is a loader failure.
- Is the webOS-version key gating correct for 4.5: mediaSei/mediaVui (version<5) rather than sei/vui? Confirm the panel accepts the mediaSei/mediaVui envelope.
- Does webOS 4.5's Load payload parser honor or REJECT the newer contents.immersive='ATMOS' key (ss4s never sends it; only newer Kodi does)? If it rejects/aborts Load, version-gate immersive off while keeping ac3PlusInfo/aacInfo.
- Frequency numeric form: Kodi sends samplerate/1000.0 (double), ss4s sends /1000 (int); identical at 48kHz but differs at 44.1kHz; which does 4.5 accept?
- Can StarfishMediaAPIs::Feed be called (off the main thread, under the new seam mutex) concurrently with the library's own GMainLoop callbacks without corrupting the g_smp object on webOS 4.5? (Step 2 correctness hinges on this.)
- Are the decompiled setContentInfo offsets (struct 0x138, ptsToDecode@0x28, SRC_TYPE_ES=7) stable across firmware, or specific to this libpf build? They are the load-bearing in-place-seek path on 4.5 and could drift on other webOS versions.
